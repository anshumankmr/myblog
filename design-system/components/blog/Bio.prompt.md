Author byline strip — avatar, name, one-line role, and Twitter handle on the sunken surface. Place under articles and at the bottom of the posts list.

```jsx
<Bio avatar="/assets/profile-pic.jpg" />
<Bio name="Anshuman Kumar" role="SWE III · AI at scale" handle="@anshuman_kmr" />
```

Composes `Avatar`. Falls back to initials when no `avatar` is given.
