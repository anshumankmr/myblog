Small uppercase pill for tags, post categories, or status chips, in the brand's accent-tint style.

```jsx
<Badge>AI / ML</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid">New</Badge>
<Badge variant="outline">Cloud</Badge>
<Badge variant="neutral">Draft</Badge>
```

- `variant`: `soft` (accent @10% bg), `solid` (filled accent), `outline` (accent hairline), `neutral` (gray).
